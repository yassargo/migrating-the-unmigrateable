**The biggest skills gap in OT security isn't technical. It's the gap between people who understand the process and people who understand the threat and the near-complete absence of people who understand both.**

ICS migration projects sit at the intersection of three worlds that don't talk to each other, don't trust each other, and don't speak the same language.

**OT Engineers** know every undocumented quirk developed over fifteen years. Their metric is uptime. Their fear: being told their systems are insecure by someone who has never operated a PLC.

**IT Security Teams** have network architecture, security tooling, compliance frameworks. Their toolkit active scanning, aggressive patching, zero-trust can crash PLCs and break control loops without OT expertise. Their assumption: IT security principles translate to OT. 

**Project Leads** bring methodology. Their risk: driving the process without understanding the operational context who satisfies neither side.

A successful migration needs all three worlds. Team composition is often the deciding factor. More than budget. More than tools.

**Four roles every project needs:**

🔹 OT Lead / Process Expert: knows what breaks and why. Must be genuinely invested — not assigned as a box-ticking exercise.  
🔹 IT Security Lead: must understand why active scanning crashes OT devices before they run one.  
🔹 Project / Compliance Lead: methodology, documentation, audit evidence. The MtU series is the toolkit for this role.  
🔹 Safety Engineer: mandatory when SIL systems are in scope. Not a project risk if absent. A safety risk.

Most projects skip the RACI. Nobody defines who is Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, Informed per activity. The OT engineer blocks a change because nobody consulted them until the night before. The IT team breaks a control loop because nobody explained the dependency. A RACI agreed before the project starts is the single most underrated tool in OT security project management. 🤝

**The honest career observation because this series is about learning in public:**

If you're an IT or security professional developing genuine OT fluency real working knowledge, not performed expertise you're positioning yourself for one of the highest-demand roles in industrial cybersecurity. CRA and NIS2 are creating demand faster than the market can supply. GICSP and IEC 62443 are the qualifications. Combined with this methodology: a genuinely differentiated profile.
