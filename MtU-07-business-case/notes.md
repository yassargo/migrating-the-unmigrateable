**The most technically sound migration project dies if it can't get funded. The reason most OT security budgets get deferred isn't the cost, it's that nobody quantified the cost of doing nothing.**

OT security budget requests fail for four consistent reasons:

**They speak engineering, not business.** "12 EOL PLCs with known CVEs" means nothing to a CFO. "12 uninsurable assets that could trigger a €10M NIS2 penalty or a shutdown costing €45K per hour" is a different conversation entirely.

**They ask for everything at once.** A €420K programme as a single line item gets deferred. The same programme in five phases with discrete risk reduction at each stage gets approved incrementally.

**They frame it as a cost.** Security investment isn't spending — it's the price of not having a much larger unplanned spend at a time and in a way you didn't choose.

**They don't quantify doing nothing.** Every business case must answer: what is the financial exposure if we don't act? That number almost always dwarfs the investment.

**Four numbers every business case needs:**

① **Hourly downtime cost**:  lost margin + fixed costs. Mid-size industrial facility: €10K–€100K/hr. Ransomware on an OT network typically causes 2–6 weeks of disruption. Do that maths and let the number sit on the page.

② **Regulatory exposure**: NIS2 penalties up to €10M or 2% of global annual turnover. Management personal liability by statute. Competent authorities across the EU are actively building enforcement capability now.

③ **Insurance position**:  underwriters are already asking detailed OT security questions. No asset inventory, no segmentation, no vulnerability management = declined coverage or elevated premiums.

④ **Risk reduction curve**:  for each phase: investment, risk retired, compliance position change. Turns a programme into discrete decisions each with a quantifiable return.

**The reframe that changes the boardroom conversation:**

Deferring this programme is not a neutral act. It is an active choice to accept the annualised risk exposure. Make that explicit.
